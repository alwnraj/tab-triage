tab-triage

Before your first sort

When you press Sort, tab-triage sends a small amount of data about each eligible tab to TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai), a third-party classification API, using your own API key. The panel below shows exactly what. Tabs that match one of your domain rules are grouped locally and are never sent.

Sorting enabled on .

What gets sent

  • Sent per tab: the site's hostname (no path, query or fragment) and the tab title cut to 120 characters.
  • Sent with every request: your group keys and descriptions below (group colours and domain lists stay local).
  • Never sent: full URLs, page content, cookies, history, tab ids; pinned tabs; tabs in incognito windows; chrome://, extension and file pages; localhost, IP addresses and intranet names; anything on your blocklist.
  • Stored locally: answers cached for 7 days under a hash key (no hostname or title stored); the API key in this browser's extension storage.
