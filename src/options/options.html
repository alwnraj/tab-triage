tab-triage

Before your first sort

When you press Sort, tab-triage sends a small amount of data about each eligible tab to the tab-triage service, which passes it to TypeSafe, a third-party classification API, and returns the answer. The service doesn't store or log it. No account or API key is needed. The panel below shows exactly what is sent; the privacy policy has the details. Titles can contain personal text (a name, an email subject), so blocklist sites whose titles you don't want sent. Tabs that match one of your domain rules are grouped locally and are never sent.

Sorting enabled on .

What gets sent

  • Sent per tab: the site's hostname (no path, query or fragment) and the tab title cut to 120 characters.
  • Sent with every request: your group keys and descriptions below (group colours and domain lists stay local).
  • Never sent: full URLs, page content, cookies, history, tab ids; pinned tabs; tabs in incognito windows; chrome://, extension and file pages; localhost, IP addresses and intranet names; anything on your blocklist.
  • Also sent: a random install id, used only for rate limiting. It isn't linked to you or your Google account.
  • Who receives it: the tab-triage service (), which forwards the hostname, title and group descriptions to TypeSafe and stores none of them.
  • Stored locally: answers cached for 7 days under a hash key (no hostname or title stored), and the last sort's results for this browser session, so the popup can show them and Undo can restore the tabs.
