Sort your tabs into groups in one click.

tab-triage files your open tabs into Chrome tab groups. Undo puts everything back exactly as it was.

Sites you know are grouped by built-in domain rules. For the rest it picks the best-fitting group, checks close calls twice, and leaves a tab alone when nothing fits.

Add to Chrome View source

Free. No account and no API key.

  • Fast

    Roughly half of a typical window is grouped on your device by domain rules, with no network call.

  • Private

    Only a site's hostname and the first 120 characters of its title are sent. Never full URLs or page content. Privacy policy

  • Yours

    Twelve default groups you can edit. Prefer it cautious? Strict mode only groups tabs it is at least 80% sure about. Open source, MIT.

1. A tab is open. It has a full address, a title and a page behind it.

2. Only the site name and the title are kept. The path, query, page content, cookies and history stay on your device.

3. Those, plus your group names, go to the service. It does not store or log them.

4. The model replies with numbers. Your browser does the grouping. Example tab; numbers are illustrative.

Not sent at all: pinned, incognito, chrome://, local or blocklisted tabs. Read the full privacy policy.

Not on the Web Store? Install from a zip. Want to run it on your own TypeSafe key? See the README. This site also hosts the small service the extension calls (/api/classify), which forwards a tab's hostname and title to TypeSafe and stores neither.
