tab-triage

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

tab-triage is a Chrome extension that sorts the open tabs in one window into Chrome tab groups. This page says exactly what it sends, to whom, and what is kept.

What leaves your browser, and when

Only when you press Sort tabs, and only after you have accepted the consent screen. For each eligible tab in that window, the extension sends:

  • the site's hostname (for example en.wikipedia.org), never the full address, path, query or fragment;
  • the tab's title, cut to 120 characters. Titles can contain personal text such as a name or an email subject, so use the blocklist for sites whose titles you don't want sent;
  • the names and descriptions of your tab groups (not their colours or domain lists);
  • a random install id created when you installed the extension. It isn't linked to you, your Google account or your browser profile, and is used only for rate limiting.

It never sends page content, full URLs, cookies, browsing history, or anything from pinned tabs, incognito windows, chrome:// and extension pages, local or intranet addresses, blocklisted sites, or tabs that match one of your domain rules (those are grouped on your device).

Who receives it

  1. The tab-triage service (this site, hosted on Vercel) receives the request, checks it, and passes the hostname, title and group descriptions to TypeSafe. It does not store or log hostnames, titles or group descriptions. For rate limiting and spending caps it keeps salted hashes (not the values themselves) of the install id and of your IP address: the per-minute counters expire after a minute, and a per-network daily usage count expires after two days. It also keeps a daily total of tokens used. Vercel, as the host, keeps standard request logs (time, path, status, IP address) for a limited period under its privacy policy.
  2. TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai) classifies the tab and returns probabilities. TypeSafe's handling of request data is described in its legal and data-handling pages.

What is kept on your device

Your settings and groups, the install id, and answers cached for 7 days under a hash of (hostname, title, groups, model version). The cached values contain only probabilities, not the hostname or title. The last sort's results and Undo state are kept in session storage and cleared when the browser closes. Clear the cache any time from the Options page; uninstalling removes everything.

What is not done

No advertising, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data, no use of data for anything except sorting your tabs, and no human review of your requests. The extension uses only the tabs, tabGroups and storage permissions, and can contact only this service.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/alwnraj/tab-triage. The source code of the extension and of this service is public there.
